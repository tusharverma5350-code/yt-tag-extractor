    What this hashtag extractor finds
    

    YouTube hashtags are words starting with # in a video's title or description. They become clickable links to a page showing other videos with the same hashtag. Up to three of them are also shown above the title. This tool scans both the title and the full description and lists every unique hashtag, ready to copy.
    

    Want the hidden keywords too? The Tags tab in the results shows the video's Studio tags, which viewers never see.

    YouTube hashtag rules you should know
    

      

    • Use more than 60 hashtags and YouTube ignores all of them for that video. It may even remove the video from search.
      • 

    • The first three hashtags in your description (if your title has none) appear above the title.
      • 

    • No spaces. #how to cook only links #how.
      • 

    • Hashtags must be relevant. Misleading or unrelated hashtags break YouTube's spam policy.
      • 

    • No hate speech, harassment or profanity in hashtags. YouTube may remove the video.
      •

    Frequently asked questions
    Where do YouTube hashtags come from?
    Creators type them in the title or description. This tool finds hashtags in both places and removes duplicates.
    How many hashtags should a YouTube video have?
    Three to five relevant hashtags usually work best. With more than 60, YouTube ignores all of them.
    Are hashtags the same as tags?
    No. Hashtags are visible and clickable. Tags are hidden keywords set in YouTube Studio. This tool shows both: hashtags in the Hashtags tab and tags in the Tags tab.
    Does it work for Shorts?
    Yes. Paste a /shorts/ link. Shorts often rely on hashtags more than tags.
