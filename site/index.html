    What is a YouTube tag extractor?
    

    Every YouTube video can have tags: keywords the creator adds in YouTube Studio to describe what the video is about. Unlike the title and description, tags are hidden from viewers. A YouTube tag extractor reads a public video's metadata and shows you those tags, so you can study what keywords successful videos in your niche are targeting.
    

    This tool goes further than most tag finders. One paste gives you the tags, every hashtag, the complete description (with all links listed separately), thumbnails in every size, view and like counts, the publish date, and a video or MP3 download.

    Do YouTube tags still matter?
    

    Honestly, less than they used to. YouTube's own help center says tags “play a minimal role in your video's discovery” and are mostly useful when your main keyword is commonly misspelled. Your title, thumbnail, description and the first 15 seconds of the video matter far more.
    

    But extracting tags is still one of the fastest ways to do keyword research:
    

      

    • Find the exact phrases top videos in your niche are built around.
      • 

    • Spot misspellings and synonyms (“tutorial” vs “tutorail”, “PS5” vs “PlayStation 5”) worth adding to your own tags.
      • 

    • Get title and description ideas. Tags often reveal the long-tail search terms a creator is aiming for.
      • 

    • Audit your own videos to check what you actually added months ago.
      • 

    


    Tips for using extracted tags
    

    Don't copy all tags blindly. Tags that don't describe your video are treated as misleading metadata under YouTube's spam policies. Keep only the ones that truly match your content.
    

      

    • Stay under 500 characters. That's YouTube's limit for all tags combined. The counter above shows where you are.
      • 

    • Put your main keyword first, then variations, then broader topic tags.
      • 

    • Use 5–15 focused tags instead of 40 loosely related ones.
      • 

    • Compare several top videos. Tags that appear across many of them are the strongest signals for that topic.
      • 

    • Many popular videos have no tags at all. If a video shows zero tags, that's real, not an error.
      •

    Frequently asked questions
    Is this YouTube tag extractor free?
    Yes. It's completely free, with no sign-up and no browser extension. Fair-use rate limits apply only to stop abuse.
    Why does a video show no tags?
    Tags are optional and many creators, including big channels, don't add any. If the tool shows zero tags, the video really has none. The hashtags and description tabs often contain the keywords instead.
    Can I see tags of any YouTube video?
    You can see the tags of any public or unlisted video you have the link to. Private videos can't be read by anyone except the owner.
    Does it work with YouTube Shorts?
    Yes. Paste a youtube.com/shorts/ link and you'll get the Short's tags, hashtags and description. See our Shorts tag extractor for Shorts-specific tips.
    How many tags can a YouTube video have?
    There's no fixed number. The limit is 500 characters for all tags combined. Our counter shows the total so you can trim your list before pasting it into YouTube Studio.
    Is it allowed to use another creator's tags?
    Tags are public metadata, so viewing them is fine. Only reuse tags that accurately describe your own video, because misleading tags go against YouTube's spam policy.
