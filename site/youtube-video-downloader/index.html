    Frequently asked questions
    Is this YouTube downloader free?
    Yes. It's free with no sign-up. Fair-use limits keep it running for everyone.
    Why is the downloaded video playing without sound?
    It shouldn't. We merge video and audio into one MP4. If you use a very old player, try VLC.
    Can I download YouTube Shorts?
    Yes. Paste the /shorts/ link and choose MP4 or MP3.
    Is downloading YouTube videos legal?
    Downloading your own videos or content you have permission for is fine. Downloading other copyrighted videos without permission goes against YouTube's Terms of Service and may break copyright law where you live.
    Why did my download fail?
    The video may be private, too long, age-restricted, or YouTube may be temporarily limiting our server. Wait a few minutes and try again.
