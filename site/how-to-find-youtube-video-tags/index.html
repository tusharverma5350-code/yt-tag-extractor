How to Find the Tags of Any YouTube Video

YouTube hides video tags from viewers, but they're still public metadata. Here are three ways to see them, from fastest to most technical.

Method 1: Use a free tag extractor (fastest)

The quickest way, and it works on phones too:

  1. Copy the video's link (on mobile: Share → Copy link).
  2. Open our YouTube Tag Extractor and paste the link.
  3. The tags appear instantly. Click Copy all tags to get them comma-separated.

You also get the video's hashtags, full description, thumbnails and stats in the same place.

Method 2: View the page source (desktop only)

  1. Open the video on youtube.com in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  2. Press Ctrl+U (Windows/Linux) or ⌥⌘U (Mac) to open the page source.
  3. Press Ctrl+F and search for "keywords".
  4. The list after "keywords":[ contains the video's tags.

It works without any tool, but it's hard to read and copy, and it doesn't work on mobile.

Method 3: YouTube Studio (your own videos)

  1. Go to studio.youtube.com → Content.
  2. Click a video → Details → Show more.
  3. The Tags field shows and lets you edit your tags.

Why some videos have no tags

Tags are optional, and YouTube itself says they play a minimal role in discovery. Many large channels skip them entirely, and videos uploaded from the phone app usually have none. If every method shows nothing, the video simply has no tags.

What to do with the tags you find

  • Compare the tags of 3–5 top videos for your topic. Repeated tags are the strongest keywords.
  • Reuse only tags that truly describe your video.
  • Keep your total under 500 characters and lead with your main keyword.
  • Spend more effort on your title, thumbnail and first 15 seconds. Those drive most views.

Frequently asked questions

Can you see tags on YouTube?

Not on the watch page. Tags are hidden from viewers. Use a tag extractor, the page source, or YouTube Studio for your own videos.

Can I find tags on my phone?

Yes. Copy the link from the YouTube app and paste it into a web-based tag extractor like ours. The page-source method doesn't work on mobile.

Are YouTube tags important in 2026?

They're a minor factor. They help mostly with misspellings and understanding context. Titles, thumbnails, descriptions and viewer retention matter much more.
