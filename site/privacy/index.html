Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

What we collect

  • Video links you submit are sent to our server to fetch public data from YouTube. We don't save them.
  • IP addresses are kept in memory for up to 10 minutes to apply rate limits, then discarded. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.
  • Downloaded files are created temporarily and deleted right after they're sent to you, or within one hour at most.

What stays on your device

Your theme choice and your last five looked-up videos (“Recent”) are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. Clear your browser data to remove them.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. If that changes, for example to show ads, we'll update this page and ask for consent where required.

Third parties

Video data and thumbnail images come from YouTube (Google). Loading thumbnails connects your browser to i.ytimg.com. See Google's privacy policy.

Questions? See the contact page.
