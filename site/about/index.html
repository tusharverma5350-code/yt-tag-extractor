About TagExtract

TagExtract is a small, free set of tools for YouTube creators and marketers. It started as a single tag extractor and grew into one place to read everything public about a video: tags, hashtags, description, links, thumbnails and stats.

Our principles

  • Free and fast. No sign-up, no browser extension, lightweight pages.
  • Honest. We tell you when tags don't matter much, instead of selling you a “tag score”.
  • Private. No accounts and no tracking cookies. See our privacy policy.

How it works

When you paste a link, our server reads the video's public metadata from YouTube and sends it back to your browser. We don't store the videos you look up.
