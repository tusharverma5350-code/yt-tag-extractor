    Frequently asked questions
    What size are YouTube thumbnails?
    The recommended upload size is 1280×720 (16:9). YouTube also creates smaller versions: 640×480, 480×360 and 320×180.
    Why is there no HD thumbnail?
    Some older or low-resolution videos never got a 1280×720 version. The tool then shows only the sizes that exist.
    Can I download a Shorts thumbnail?
    Yes. Paste the /shorts/ link. Shorts thumbnails are usually a frame picked from the video.
    Is it free?
    Yes. It's free and needs no sign-up. Images download straight to your device as JPG files.
